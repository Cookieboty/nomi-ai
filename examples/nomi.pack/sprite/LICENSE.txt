Nomi·诺米 mascot artwork

Owner-provided, AI-generated artwork, licensed by the project owner.
The project owner (Cookieboty) created this artwork with AI image tools and
grants its use in the Nomi-ai repository under the repository's MIT license.

The layered rig in layers/ (body, head, side hair, ribbon, eyes, mouth and the
painted closed/happy-eye and open/o/frown-mouth variants) was cut from the
owner's clean half-body illustration with scripts/brand/build-nomi-rig.py
(local tools only; occluded areas inpainted). Same licence as above.

The head layers (head, crown, bangs, side hair, ribbon, eye white/iris/lashes,
mouths) come from the owner's front-facing HD head image
(also owner-provided, AI-generated), cut with scripts/brand/build-nomi-head-hd.py.
Same licence as above.

The body parts (torso, sleeves, badge, strap) are split from the same body layer
with scripts/brand/build-nomi-body-parts.py (hidden areas inpainted locally);
the neck/collar join is then finished with scripts/brand/polish-nomi-neck.py.
Same licence as above.

Brand parts repainted from the official closeup (hairclips, orange bow, earring,
choker charm) were cut from the official art itself with
scripts/brand/fix-nomi-brand-parts.py (crops only, no generative redraw).
Same licence as above.

layers/hands/*.png are AI-generated, non-brand hand / forearm pieces, cut out
and colour-matched to the master with scripts/brand/build-nomi-hands.py,
scripts/brand/build-nomi-hands-extra.py (prayer / OK / chest) and
scripts/brand/build-nomi-arm-parts.py. Licensed by the project owner under the
repository's MIT license. They contain no brand-bearing detail.

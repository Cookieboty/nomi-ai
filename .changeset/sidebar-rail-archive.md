feat(sidebar): 侧栏改为「固定导航栏 + 会话面板」：导航栏放对话 / 任务 / 素材库，工作台模式下竖排列出全部端点，设置在最下面；会话面板顶部用「Nomi 助手 ⌄ / Nomi 工作台 ⌄」切换模式；收起后悬停「对话」或端点弹出会话浮层。会话行不再显示时间，悬停弹出详情卡片（标题、端点、时间、状态、工作区）。Nomi 对话可以归档（在「设置 → 通用 → 已归档的对话」里恢复或永久删除），永久删除放进「更多」并再确认一次。工作区不再放在对话顶栏，改在新会话的输入框上方选择（可搜索、可不使用）。/ The sidebar is now a fixed rail plus a chat panel: the rail holds Chats / Tasks / Assets, lists every endpoint in workbench mode and keeps Settings at the bottom; a "Nomi Assistant ⌄ / Nomi Workbench ⌄" menu switches modes; when collapsed, hovering Chats or an endpoint floats the chat panel. Rows no longer show the time; hovering shows a detail card (title, endpoint, time, status, workspace). Nomi chats can be archived (restore or delete them in Settings → General → Archived chats) and permanent delete moved into "More" with a confirmation. The workspace moved from the chat header to a bar above the composer of a new chat (searchable, can be cleared).

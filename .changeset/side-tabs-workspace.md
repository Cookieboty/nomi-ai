feat(chat): 对话旁改成一排标签：子任务和打开的文件共用一块区域，可以同时开好几个子任务，切换时各自的进度、草稿和滚动位置都保留；双击任意标签铺满整个对话区，主会话变成第一个不能关的标签，再双击或按 Esc 回到分栏；焦点在标签区时 Ctrl+Tab 切换、⌘/Ctrl+W 关闭当前标签，开着的标签按会话记住。/ The column beside the chat is now a tab strip shared by sub-tasks and opened files: several sub-tasks can stay open at once and keep their progress, draft and scroll position when you switch; double-click any tab to fill the whole chat area with the main chat as a first, non-closable tab, and double-click again or press Esc to return to the split view; with focus in the tab area, Ctrl+Tab switches and ⌘/Ctrl+W closes the current tab, and open tabs are remembered per conversation.
